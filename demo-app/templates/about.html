About

Brewly opened in 2021 in what used to be a tailor's shop. The cutting table is still here; it's the long one by the window, and it seats six if nobody brings a laptop bag.

There are four of us. We started roasting our own beans because we wanted to know exactly how old they were. Most weeks we buy from two estates in Chikmagalur for the house blend, plus one coffee from further away for the pour-over.

We don't do syrups and we don't have Wi-Fi. We do have a water bowl for dogs, a shelf of books you can borrow, and the same playlist we've had since the first week, which a few regulars have asked us to change.
